# Voice — High Point Renovation & Roofing

## Brand Identity
- **Company:** High Point Renovation & Roofing
- **Owner:** Terry Preston (owner-operator)
- **Location:** Kennewick, WA (Tri-Cities, Eastern Washington)
- **Register:** `family` — plain-spoken, honest, owner-led; no corporate distance, no hype
- **Tone:** Direct, trustworthy, practical. Terry talks to homeowners the way a knowledgeable neighbor would — no pressure, no jargon, no invented urgency.

## Voice Principles
1. **Owner-first.** Terry is the face of every job. Copy names him when relevant and reinforces that the person who inspects is the person who manages the work.
2. **Honest over impressive.** Never oversell. If a repair will hold, say so. If it won't, say that too. The brand's reputation is built on not upselling.
3. **Plain language.** Short sentences. Active voice. Avoid roofing jargon unless immediately explained.
4. **Local and specific.** Reference the Tri-Cities climate (high-desert sun, Columbia River wind, day-to-night temperature swings) and specific neighborhoods when relevant. Generic copy is off-brand.
5. **Written estimate = final price.** This is a core brand promise. Copy must reinforce it, never undermine it.
6. **No pressure.** Avoid urgency tactics ("act now", "limited time"). The brand earns trust by not pushing.

## Sample Phrases (on-brand)
- "The same owner who inspects your roof personally manages and oversees the crew that builds it."
- "The written estimate is the price you pay — no surprise line items."
- "If a repair will hold, we tell you. If it won't, we tell you that too."
- "Free inspection, free written estimate, no obligation to move forward."
- "Built for high-desert sun, Columbia River wind, and big day-to-night temperature swings."

## Anti-Patterns (never use)
- Superlatives without evidence ("the best roofer in the Tri-Cities", "#1 contractor")
- Invented urgency ("call before prices go up", "limited slots")
- Vague promises ("we'll take care of everything")
- Capstone/Kansas City references (template artifact — never leak through)
- Specific price quotes in copy (use "general market range" framing only)
